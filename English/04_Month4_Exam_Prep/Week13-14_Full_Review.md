## NGÀY 1–2: ÔN THÌ ĐỘNG TỪ

### Bài ôn nhanh – 50 câu trắc nghiệm

**Điền vào chỗ trống với thì đúng:**

1. She ___ (work) at a hospital for 5 years. → **has worked**
2. I ___ (not/see) that film yet. → **haven't seen**
3. We ___ (have) dinner when the power went off. → **were having**
4. He ___ (call) you as soon as he arrives. → **will call**
5. They ___ (just/finish) the project. → **have just finished**
6. Look! It ___ (rain). → **is raining**
7. I ___ (visit) my grandparents every Sunday. → **visit**
8. She ___ (go) to Paris last year. → **went**
9. ___ you ever ___ (eat) sushi? → **Have... eaten**
10. He ___ (study) hard these days. → **is studying**

### Nhận diện thì qua dấu hiệu:

| Dấu hiệu | Thì |
|----------|-----|
| yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020 | Quá khứ đơn |
| now, at the moment, look!, listen! | Hiện tại tiếp diễn |
| always, usually, every day | Hiện tại đơn |
| already, yet, ever, never, just, for, since | HTHT |
| tomorrow, next week, soon | Tương lai |
| when... (2 hành động QK song song) | QKTD + QKĐ |

## NGÀY 3–4: ÔN MODALS & SO SÁNH

### Modals – Chọn đúng

| Tình huống | Modal |
|-----------|-------|
| Đưa lời khuyên | should |
| Bắt buộc tuyệt đối | must |
| Không cần thiết | don't have to |
| Cấm hoàn toàn | mustn't |
| Xin phép trang trọng | may / could |
| Khả năng hiện tại | can |
| Có thể xảy ra (~50%) | may |
| Có thể xảy ra (~30%) | might |

### So sánh – Bài tập nhanh

Hoàn thành câu với dạng đúng:
1. This coffee is ___ (hot) than that one. → **hotter**
2. She is the ___ (good) student in the class. → **best**
3. English is ___ (difficult) than Math. → **more difficult**
4. He is not as ___ (tall) as his brother. → **tall**
5. Which city is ___ (big) in Vietnam? → **the biggest**

## NGÀY 7: ÔN CÂU ĐIỀU KIỆN & MẠCH LẠC

### Câu điều kiện – Bài tập

Chọn loại điều kiện và điền đúng:
1. If it ___ (snow) in Hanoi, I ___ (be) very surprised. (khó xảy ra)
   → If it **snowed** in Hanoi, I **would be** very surprised. (Loại 2)

2. If you ___ (study) hard, you ___ (pass) the exam. (có thể xảy ra)
   → If you **study** hard, you **will pass** the exam. (Loại 1)

3. If you ___ (mix) red and yellow, you ___ (get) orange.
   → If you **mix** red and yellow, you **get** orange. (Loại 0)

### Từ nối – Điền phù hợp

Điền từ nối thích hợp:
1. I was tired. ___, I didn't stop studying. (**However**)
2. She loves music. ___, she plays the guitar. (**Furthermore** / **In addition**)
3. He studied hard. ___, he passed the exam. (**As a result** / **Therefore**)
4. ___ it was raining, we went out. (**Although** / **Even though**)

## 📝 BÀI KIỂM TRA TỔNG HỢP CUỐI THÁNG 3

### Grammar Test – 40 câu

Làm trong 30 phút, sau đó kiểm tra đáp án:

**Chọn đáp án đúng (Câu 1–20):**

1. She ___ English for 3 years.
   - A) learns B) learned C) **has been learning** D) is learning

2. By the time he arrived, she ___ left.
   - A) had **already** B) already C) **had already** D) has already

3. The book ___ by millions of people worldwide.
   - A) reads B) **is read** C) reading D) was reading

4. If only I ___ more time!
   - A) have B) **had** C) would have D) will have

5. She suggested ___ a break.
   - A) to take B) take C) **taking** D) took

6. Not only ___ late, but he also forgot his homework.
   - A) was he **came** B) he came C) **did he come** D) he did come

7. The ___ the temperature, the more uncomfortable we feel.
   - A) high B) higher **→ correct** C) highest D) highly

8. I wish I ___ fly like a bird.
   - A) can B) will C) **could** D) would

9. He ___ be tired – he's been working since 6 AM.
   - A) should B) could **→** C) **must** D) might

10. The meeting ___ for 3 hours when the director finally arrived.
    - A) was going B) has been going C) **had been going** D) went

**Đáp án:** 1-C, 2-C, 3-B, 4-B, 5-C, 6-C, 7-B, 8-C, 9-C, 10-C
