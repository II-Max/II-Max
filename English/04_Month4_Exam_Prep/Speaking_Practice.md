# 🎤 THÁNG 4 – Speaking: Luyện Nói Cho Bài Thi EPT

> **Mục tiêu:** Tự tin giao tiếp trong Bài thi Speaking 3 vòng tại Little UK (LUK)

---

## 🎯 HIỂU RÕ 3 VÒNG THI SPEAKING

### Vòng 1: Giao Tiếp Cơ Bản (Basic Communication)
- Tự giới thiệu bản thân
- Hỏi đáp câu hỏi YES/NO và WH-
- Chủ đề: Bản thân, gia đình, học tập, sở thích

### Vòng 2: Phonics (Phát Âm)
- Đọc từ/câu theo yêu cầu
- Kiểm tra phát âm chuẩn, trọng âm, nối âm

### Vòng 3: Phỏng Vấn Trực Tiếp
- Cuộc hội thoại dài hơn với giảng viên
- Chủ đề phức tạp hơn (kế hoạch, ý kiến)
- Thể hiện khả năng giải thích và lập luận

---

## 1. TỰ GIỚI THIỆU BẢN THÂN (1 phút)

### Mẫu tự giới thiệu chuẩn:

```
"Hello, my name is [Tên]. 
I am [tuổi] years old. 
I am from [thành phố], Vietnam.

I am a first-year student at FPT University, 
and I am studying [ngành].

In my free time, I like [sở thích 1] and [sở thích 2]. 
I also enjoy [hoạt động khác].

My goal is to [mục tiêu]. 
I am very excited to start my studies here.

Thank you."
```

### Ví dụ hoàn chỉnh:
```
"Hello, my name is Minh Duc Pham.
I am 18 years old.
I am from Hanoi, Vietnam.

I am a first-year student at FPT University,
and I am studying Information Technology.

In my free time, I like playing football and listening to music.
I also enjoy watching movies – especially action films.

My goal is to become a software engineer in the future.
I am very excited to start my studies here.

Thank you for listening."
```

## 2. CÁC CÂU HỎI THƯỜNG GẶP – VÒNG 1

### Chủ đề: Bản thân

**Q: What is your name?**
> A: My name is [Tên]. / I am [Tên].

**Q: How old are you?**
> A: I am [tuổi] years old.

**Q: Where are you from?**
> A: I am from [thành phố], Vietnam.

**Q: What do you study?**
> A: I am studying [ngành] at FPT University.

**Q: Do you have any siblings?**
> A: Yes, I have [one brother / two sisters]. / No, I am an only child.

**Q: How long have you lived in Hanoi?**
> A: I have lived here for [X] years. / I was born here.

---

### Chủ đề: Học tập

**Q: What is your favorite subject?**
> A: My favorite subject is [môn]. I like it because it is [interesting / challenging / useful].

**Q: Why did you choose FPT University?**
> A: I chose FPT University because it has a great [program / reputation]. 
> Also, I want to [learn English / work in technology].

**Q: How do you usually study?**
> A: I usually study at home in the evening. I spend about [X] hours a day studying.

---

### Chủ đề: Sở thích

**Q: What do you do in your free time?**
> A: In my free time, I usually [hoạt động]. I enjoy it because [lý do].

**Q: Do you like music? What kind?**
> A: Yes, I love music! I especially like [Pop / K-pop / Vietnamese music] because [lý do].

**Q: Are you good at sports?**
> A: Yes, I love [football / badminton]. I play it [every weekend / with my friends].
> / No, I'm not very sporty, but I like walking.

---

### Chủ đề: Ý kiến & Kế hoạch

**Q: What are your plans after university?**
> A: After I graduate, I want to [work as a... / start my own business / go abroad].
> I am going to [prepare for... / apply for...].

**Q: What do you think about learning English?**
> A: I think English is very important because it helps me [communicate with people / find a good job / access more information].
> It is challenging but I really enjoy learning it.

## 3. BÀI TẬP PHONICS – VÒNG 2

### Nhóm từ dễ đọc sai

**Âm TH:**
- the, this, that, these, those, there, their, them, then, though
- think, thank, three, through, throw, throat, thunder, theater

**Âm cuối L:**
- school, cool, pool, tall, small, call, fall, ball, pull, full

**Phân biệt /v/ và /b/:**
- very, video, visit, voice, van, view, value, village

**Âm /w/ và /v/:**
- west, win, wet, want, word, work → /w/
- vest, vine, vote, vet, veil → /v/

**Phân biệt -ed:**
- /t/: stopped, worked, watched, laughed
- /d/: lived, called, opened, enjoyed
- /ɪd/: wanted, needed, started, landed

### Câu luyện phát âm (Tongue Twisters)
1. *"She sells seashells by the seashore."*
2. *"Three thin thieves thought thoughtful thoughts."*
3. *"Red lorry, yellow lorry."*
4. *"Betty Botter bought some butter."*
5. *"How much wood would a woodchuck chuck?"*

## 4. CÁC CÂU HỎI VÀ TRẢ LỜI MẪU – VÒNG 3

### Chủ đề: Công nghệ

**Q: Do you use social media? Which ones?**
> A: Yes, I use Facebook and TikTok every day. I use them to 
> connect with friends and watch videos for entertainment.
> However, I try not to spend too much time on them because 
> it can be a distraction from studying.

### Chủ đề: Môi trường

**Q: What can young people do to help the environment?**
> A: I think there are several things we can do. 
> First, we should reduce plastic use – for example, bring a reusable bag 
> when shopping. Second, we can save electricity by turning off lights 
> when we leave a room. Finally, using public transport instead of 
> motorbikes helps reduce air pollution.

### Chủ đề: Cuộc sống đô thị

**Q: What are the advantages and disadvantages of living in a big city?**
> A: Living in a big city like Hanoi has many advantages. 
> There are more job opportunities and better schools and hospitals. 
> However, there are also disadvantages. The traffic is very heavy 
> and the cost of living is quite high. Also, it can be noisy and 
> stressful compared to the countryside.

## 5. KỸ NĂNG GIAO TIẾP QUAN TRỌNG

### Khi không hiểu câu hỏi:
- *"I'm sorry, could you repeat that, please?"*
- *"Could you say that more slowly?"*
- *"I'm not sure I understand. Do you mean...?"*

### Khi cần thêm thời gian suy nghĩ:
- *"That's a good question..."*
- *"Let me think for a moment..."*
- *"Well, I think..."*
- *"Hmm, that's interesting..."*

### Khi không biết từ:
- *"I don't know the exact word, but it means..."*
- *"How can I say... it's like a..."*
- *"I'm not sure of the word, but I think it's similar to..."*

## 7. TỪ VỰNG HỮU ÍCH CHO SPEAKING

### Diễn đạt ý kiến:
- I think / I believe / In my opinion...
- I agree / I disagree with...
- From my point of view...
- As far as I'm concerned...

### Liên kết ý:
- First of all, / Firstly, / To begin with,
- In addition, / Furthermore, / Moreover,
- On the other hand, / However,
- Finally, / To sum up, / In conclusion,

### Nhấn mạnh:
- especially, particularly, definitely
- really, absolutely, totally
- what's more, above all

## 8. MÔ PHỎNG PHỎNG VẤN SPEAKING EPT

### Script mô phỏng Vòng 1:

**Giám khảo:** Hello, please come in and sit down. My name is Mr. John. What's your name?  
**Bạn:** Hello, my name is Minh Duc Pham. Nice to meet you.

**Giám khảo:** Nice to meet you too, Minh. Where are you from?  
**Bạn:** I'm from Hanoi, Vietnam. I was born and raised here.

**Giám khảo:** Can you tell me about your family?  
**Bạn:** Sure! I live with my parents and one younger sister. My father is an engineer and my mother is a teacher. We are a small but happy family.

**Giám khảo:** Why did you choose to study at FPT University?  
**Bạn:** I chose FPT University because it has an excellent reputation, especially in technology and English training. I believe studying here will help me achieve my goals.

**Giám khảo:** What are your hobbies?  
**Bạn:** I really enjoy playing football with my friends on weekends. I also like listening to music and watching movies in English to improve my language skills.

**Giám khảo:** That's great! Thank you, Minh.  
**Bạn:** Thank you very much. It was a pleasure talking to you.
